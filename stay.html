Wine Country

Where to Stay

Whether you’re joining us for the celebrations or making a longer getaway out of your trip, here are a few of our favorite places to stay. We recommend booking early, as May is one of the most popular times to visit wine country.

Our Host Hotel

The Lodge at Sonoma Resort

Marriott Autograph Collection

Our home for the entire wedding weekend is The Lodge at Sonoma and we’d love for you to stay with us!

Just five minutes from our wedding venue, The Lodge is where we’ll kick off the weekend with our welcome party and gather one last time for our farewell brunch. Staying here with us means you’ll be right in the middle of the wedding weekend, from the planned celebrations to all the spontaneous moments in between.

Nestled among lush gardens, winding pathways, and beautiful wine country landscaping, The Lodge feels like a peaceful retreat while still being just minutes from Sonoma Plaza.

We strongly encourage you to book your stay at The Lodge as soon as possible. Rooms are limited, and booking directly with the hotel is the best way to secure your room.
