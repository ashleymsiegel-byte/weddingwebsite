What to Wear

Golden Hour Formal

We love any excuse to get dressed up, and our wedding feels like the perfect one. We hope you’ll join us in celebrating with a look that’s elegant, romantic, and ready for a golden evening in wine country.

Picture golden hour among the vineyards. Rich browns, warm neutrals, earthy greens, soft peaches, golden yellows, and other warm, romantic tones are all beautiful places to start. We encourage you to embrace our palette and make it your own.

One color caveat: please leave white, ivory, and off-white outfits for the couple. Tan, beige, and other warm neutrals, including lighter shades, are absolutely welcome. When in doubt, just make sure it reads more golden hour than wedding white. We thank you in advance.

Note: our ceremony and celebration will take place outdoors, so please keep grass in mind when choosing footwear. Sonoma evenings can cool down quickly — we recommend bringing a layer for later in the evening.
